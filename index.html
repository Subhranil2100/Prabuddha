What is PRABUDDHA?

PRABUDDHA 2026 is the flagship technical and innovation festival of Techno International New Town, bringing together students, developers, and innovators from across the country.

Our History

Since its inception, PRABUDDHA has grown into one of Eastern India's most prestigious college tech fests, hosting 500+ participants, 20+ events, and industry-leading guest mentorship sessions each year.

    FAQ

    Who can participate? ▼
    Any student currently enrolled in an undergraduate or postgraduate program from any recognized college or university in India.
    Is there a registration fee? ▼
    Most events are free to register. Certain advanced workshops may have a nominal fee. Details are mentioned on each event card.
    Can I register for multiple events? ▼
    Yes! You can select multiple events during registration as long as their timeslots do not overlap.
    Will certificates be provided? ▼
    Yes, all registered participants will receive a digital participation certificate after the fest. Winners receive special achievement certificates and trophies.
    How do I get to the venue? ▼
    TINT is located in Action Area II, Newtown, Kolkata and is accessible by metro, bus, and cab. Detailed directions are available on the college website.
